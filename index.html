Happy Anniversary, Bujjithalli ❤️

First of all, nenu chesina prathi daniki, ninnu hurt chesina prathi sari, I am really, really sorry ra.

Mana special day ni happy ga cheyyalekapoyinanduku, ee roju nee pakkana undalekapoyinanduku nannu kshaminchu ra. Nuvvu expect chesina happiness ni ee roju neeku ivvalekapoyanu ani naaku chala badha ga undi.

Nanna, mana bond lo chala ups and downs unnayi. Chala happy moments unnayi, alage chala sad moments kuda unnayi. Konni rojulu manam chala happy ga unnam, inkonni rojulu chinna chinna vishayalake godavalu paddam.

Kani mana journey lo nenu chala tappulu chesanu. Ninnu kotina rojulu unnayi, ninnu matalatho hurt chesina rojulu unnayi. Appudu nee feelings ni artham chesukolekapoyanu. Nenu chesina vatiki excuses cheppalenu ra. Ninnu ala hurt cheyyadam assalu correct kaadu. You always deserved love, respect and care from me. I am genuinely sorry for all the pain I caused you.

Eeroju neeku oka small gift kuda koni ivvalekapoyanu. Mana anniversary roju neeku oka beautiful surprise ivvali, nee face lo smile chudali ani anukunna. Kani situation ala lekapovadam valla adi kuda cheyyalekapoyanu. And I am really sorry for that ra.

Nijam cheppalante, neetho chala cheppali ani undi. Chala feelings unnayi, chala memories gurthostunnayi. Kani ippudu literally naaku matalu ravatledu. Em cheppalo, ekkada start cheyyalo kuda artham avatledu.

Sometimes words saripovu anipistundi. Nenu sorry cheppadam kanna, naa actions tho nenu entha change avvagalano chupinchali. Ninnu hurt chesina things repeat cheyyakunda, nee feelings ni respect chesthu, neeku peace and happiness iche person ga undali ani korukuntunna.

Mana madhya jarigina prathi danini marchipovali ani ninnu adaganu. Nenu chesina tappulani ignore cheyyamani kuda adaganu. Kani nenu better person ga maradaniki nijanga try chesthanu. Nuvvu deserve chese love ni words lo matrame kaakunda, naa actions lo kuda chupinchali.

2023 lo start aina mana journey lo ee roju oka special day. Enni memories create chesukunnamo, enni moments share chesukunnamo, anni naaku chala special ra.

One thing I want you to know is that, ee message lo unna prathi word naa heart nunchi vachindi. Naa feelings anni perfect ga words lo cheppalekapoyina, I hope you can feel how much this day means to me.

Once again, Happy Anniversary, Chittithalli 🤎
